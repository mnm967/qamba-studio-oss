# Camera grammar (official H3 vocabulary — use verbatim in beats)

Write camera as **motion type + amplitude + speed** in natural prose. Add
amplitude/speed only when meaningful; medium/normal are omitted.

Motion types (canonical): Zoom In / Zoom Out · Push In / Pull Out ·
Pan Left / Pan Right · Truck Left / Truck Right · Tilt Up / Tilt Down ·
Pedestal Up / Pedestal Down · Arc Shot · Tracking Shot · Static Shot ·
Shake Slightly / Shake Strongly · POV · Roll Clockwise / Roll Counterclockwise.

Amplitude: `with small amplitude` / `with large amplitude`.
Speed: `at slow speed` / `at fast speed`.

Written inside the action, not stacked as labels:
- "The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward her hands."
- "The camera pans right with large amplitude at fast speed, revealing the doorway."
- "The camera holds a static shot as the runner exits the frame."
- "A tracking shot follows him along the alley at fast speed."

Cuts: a new shot must introduce new information (subject, space, state,
viewpoint, time). If only distance or slight angle changes, prefer camera
motion within the shot. Shot sizes still matter: wide / medium / close-up /
extreme close-up / two-shot / over-shoulder, plus angle (eye level, low,
high, overhead, dutch).

Choreography note (fights, dance, parkour): one clear physical exchange per
beat — intention (weight shift, guard) → strike/move → impact frame →
consequence. Alternate wide geography shots with close impacts; return to a
wide whenever positions change. Speed contrast sells force: a held breath,
then the burst, optionally "Shake Slightly" on impacts.
